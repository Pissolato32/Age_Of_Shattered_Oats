| Route | Time | Notes |
|-------|:----:|-------|

**Regional Climate Notes:**
- **Dentes de Gelo:** 15-25°C colder than Valenfort year-round. Permafrost.
- **Capital (planícies):** 5-8°C warmer than Valenfort. Milder winters.
- **Costa Leste (Porto Velho):** Maritime climate. 3-5°C milder. More precipitation.
- **Alveria (Norte):** Similar to Valenfort but more coastal influence.
- **Estepe (Leste):** Dry continental. Hot summers, very cold winters.

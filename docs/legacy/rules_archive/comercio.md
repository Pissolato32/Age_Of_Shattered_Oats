## PART 72: TRADE & CARAVAN SYSTEM (Per Patch 7.6)

### 72.1 Core Principle

**The Merchant's Equation:** Profit = (Sale Price - Purchase Price) × Volume - Operating Costs - Risk Premium

### 72.2 Caravan Profit Factors

| Factor | Effect on Profit |
|--------|-----------------|
| Journey Time | Longer = higher profit: +5% per week of travel (base) |
| Goods Type | Different demand curves (see §72.4) |
| Quality Tier | Common: 1.0×, High-Grade: 1.5×, Superb: 2.0× |
| Volume | More goods = more profit, but market saturation risk |
| Regional Demand | Each region craves different goods (see §72.5) |
| Risk | +10-50% based on route security |

### 72.3 Base Value Multiplier by Journey Time

**Formula: Base Multiplier = 1.0 + (0.05 × Weeks of Travel)**

| Journey Duration | Base Value Multiplier | Example |
|-----------------|----------------------|---------|
| Local (1-3 days) | 1.1× | Village to neighboring village |
| Short (4-7 days) | 1.2× | Village to town |
| Medium (1-2 weeks) | 1.4× | Town to regional city |
| Long (2-4 weeks) | 1.8× | Cross-region trade |
| Extended (1-2 months) | 2.5× | North-South continental trade |
| Epic (3+ months) | 4.0× | Entire continent crossing |

### 72.6 Quality Multipliers

| Quality | Base Price Modifier | Luxury/Strategic Modifier | Rarity Premium |
|---------|-------------------|--------------------------|---------------|
| Common | 1.0× | 1.0× | None |
| High-Grade | 1.5× | 2.0× for luxury/strategic | +10% if sold far from source |
| Superb | 2.0× | 4.0× for luxury/strategic | +25% if sold far from source |

**Complete Formula:** Final Price = Base Price × Demand Multiplier × Journey Multiplier × Quality Multiplier × Rarity Premium

**Example — Superb Furs from Eastern Forests sold in Central Plains:**
- Base: 15 SD
- Demand: Central Plains craves furs (×1.5)
- Journey: 3 weeks (×1.15)
- Quality: Superb (×2.0)
- Rarity: Premium (×1.25)
- **Final Price: 15 × 1.5 × 1.15 × 2.0 × 1.25 = 64.7 SD per SU**

### 72.8 Caravan Operating Costs

| Cost Type | Amount | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Guards | 1 SD per 10 guards per week | Minimum 10 guards per caravan |
| Wagons | 1 SD per wagon per week | Each wagon carries 20 SU |
| Drivers | 1 SD per 2 wagons per week | 1 driver per 2 wagons |
| Tolls | Varies by route | 1-10 SD per border/bridge |
| Bribes | Optional | 5-50 SD for safe passage |
| Contingency | 10% of cargo value | Losses to bandits, spoilage, etc. |

**Typical Caravan Composition:**

| Caravan Size | Wagons | Guards | Drivers | Capacity (SU) | Weekly Cost |
|-------------|--------|--------|---------|--------------|-----------|
| Small | 5 | 10 | 3 | 100 | 23 SD/week |
| Medium | 10 | 20 | 5 | 200 | 45 SD/week |
| Large | 20 | 40 | 10 | 400 | 90 SD/week |
| Massive | 50 | 100 | 25 | 1,000 | 225 SD/week |

### 72.9 Complete Caravan Profit Formula

| Step | Calculation |
|------|------------|
| Step 1 | Purchase Cost = Σ (Goods Volume × Local Price) |
| Step 2 | Sale Value = Σ (Goods Volume × Destination Price) |
| Step 3 | Total Costs = (Weekly Cost × Weeks Travel) + Tolls + Bribes + Contingency |
| Step 4 | Gross Profit = Sale Value — Purchase Cost |
| Step 5 | Net Profit = Gross Profit — Total Costs |
| Step 6 | Margin % = (Net Profit ÷ Purchase Cost) × 100 |

### 72.10 Caravan Profit Examples

#### Example 1 — Local Grain Trade
- Route: Village to Town (3 days, 0.5 weeks)
- Goods: 100 SU Grain (Common)
- Purchase: 100 SU × 1 SD = 100 SD
- Destination Price: Town needs grain (×1.2) × journey (×1.025) = 1.23 SD
- Sale Value: 100 × 1.23 = 123 SD
- Operating Costs: Small caravan, 0.5 weeks = 12 SD
- **Net Profit: 11 SD (11% margin)**

#### Example 2 — Cross-Region Luxury Trade
- Route: Eastern Forests (North) to Central Plains Capital (3 weeks)
- Goods: 50 SU Superb Furs
- Purchase: 15 SD × Superb (×2.0) × local supply (-15%) = 25.5 SD/SU
- Total Purchase: 50 × 25.5 = 1,275 SD
- Destination Price: 15 × 1.5 × 1.15 × 2.0 × 1.25 = 64.7 SD/SU
- Sale Value: 50 × 64.7 = 3,235 SD
- Operating Costs: 135 SD + tolls (20) + contingency (324) = 479 SD
- **Net Profit: 1,481 SD (116% margin)**

#### Example 3 — Strategic Resources in Wartime
- Route: Southern Mountains to Northern Snowlands (6 weeks)
- Goods: 100 SU High-Grade Iron
- Purchase: 5 SD × High-Grade (×1.5) × local supply (-20%) = 6 SD/SU
- Total Purchase: 100 × 6 = 600 SD
- Destination Price: 5 × 2.0 × 1.3 × 1.5 = 19.5 SD/SU
- Sale Value: 100 × 19.5 = 1,950 SD
- Operating Costs: Large caravan 6wk (540) + bribes (100) + contingency (195) = 835 SD
- **Net Profit: 515 SD (86% margin, high risk)**

### 72.11 Caravan Risk Table

Each week, roll **1d6** (standard routes) or **1d6+1** (dangerous routes).

| Roll | Incident | Effect |
|------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Bandit attack | Lose 1d10% of cargo, 1d6 guards killed |
| 2 | Weather delay | +1 week travel, +1d6×10% costs |
| 3 | Broken wagon | Lose 1d3 days, repair costs 5 SD |
| 4 | River crossing issue | Toll doubled or 1 week delay |
| 5 | Guard dispute | Pay 1d6×5 SD bonus or they leave |
| 6 | Smooth travel | No incident |
| 7+ | Disaster! | Roll twice on this table |

## PART 74: GOODS TRACKING SYSTEM (Per Patch 7.0, 7.7, 7.14)

### 74.1 Core Rule

All goods are tracked in **Supply Units (SU)** inside your ledger.

### 74.2 Goods Classification

**FOOD GOODS** (feed people & armies) — Convert into Food Supply Units (FSU):
- Grain, Fish, Salted Meat, Dried Fruit, Cheese, Livestock
- **Conversion: 100 SU of Food Goods = 1 FSU**

**TRADE GOODS** (wealth & war industry) — Cannot feed troops:
- Iron, Timber, Salt, Wine, Cloth, Horses, Silver

### 74.5 Resource Quality & Crafting Bonuses (Per Patch 6.4)

Crafting with quality materials gives combat bonuses for Iron and Tar.

| Quality | Price Multiplier | Crafting Bonus |
|---------|-----------------|----------------|
| Common | 1.0× | None |
| High-Grade | 1.5× | 1 reroll per battle |
| Superb | 2.0× | 2 rerolls per battle |

- **Weapons/Ammunition:** Rerolls per battle
- **Armor:** High-quality = ignore first successful hit (High-Grade: 1 hit, Superb: 2 hits)
- **Tar:** Grants +1 to hit (High-Grade) or +2 to hit (Superb), or +1d6 to attack pool
- **Mixed quality:** Item defaults to lowest quality used

### 74.6 Finished Goods Tracking (Per Patch 7.7)

All finished weapons, armor, and mounts are tracked as distinct items with location, quality, and history. When a soldier is equipped, the specific item is removed from inventory and tracked to that unit.

### 74.7 Equipment Persistence Rules (Per Patch 7.7)

- **No Item Spawning:** Everything placed during world generation
- **Crafted Items Enter Inventory** when smith finishes
- **Imported Items Enter Inventory** when caravan arrives
- **Looted Items Enter Inventory** after battle
- **Equipped Items Are Tracked** to specific soldiers
- **Inherited Items** exist from character creation

## PART 79: WORLD ECONOMIC RESPONSE (Per Patch 7.0)

### 79.1 Lords Notice Wealth

If treasury exceeds **10,000 SD**:
- Neighboring lords demand loans/tribute
- Bandit activity increases
- Mercenary prices increase

### 79.2 Lords Notice Weakness

If treasury drops below **500 SD**:
- Vassals become rebellious
- Neighbors press claims
- Trade partners demand upfront payment

### 79.3 Economic Ripples

- New iron mine creates jobs → population grows → more taxes
- Failed harvest causes famine → refugees flee → labor pool elsewhere increases
- Sacked city floods market with loot → prices drop → local producers suffer

### 85.2 Succession Order
1. Eldest legitimate child
2. Younger children
3. Siblings of the ruler
4. Children of siblings
5. Closest blood relative
If no clear heir — Succession Crisis begins.

### 85.3 Claim Creation
| Source | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Blood Claim | Blood relation to title holder |
| Marriage Claim | Marriage creates future claims through children |
| War Claim | Conquest or political justification |

### 85.4 Claim Strength
| Strength | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Strong Claim | Direct heir, eldest child, uncontested bloodline |
| Moderate Claim | Nephew or cousin, marriage descendant |
| Weak Claim | Ancient claim, distant bloodline, political justification |

### 85.5 Claim Persistence
Claims do not expire. A house may press a claim generations later, though strength may diminish over time.

### 85.6 Succession Crisis — Triggers
- Ruler dies with no living children
- Ruler dies with no living siblings
- All potential heirs underage (under 16) with no regent
- Multiple claimants with equal strength
- Late ruler's will disputed or missing
- Assassination with no clear perpetrator

**Immediate Effects:**
- All loyalty scores to late ruler become **0**
- All vassals must choose sides within **1d6 weeks**
- Realm Opinion of all factions drops by **-2**
- Trade routes through region **-50%** effectiveness
- Neighboring realms gain claim to border territories

### 85.7 Civil War Phases
| Phase | Duration | Effects |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Phase 1 — Disputed Succession | Weeks 1-4 | Loyalty checks daily, no central authority, -1 Opinion all factions, Trade -25% |
| Phase 2 — Open Conflict | Weeks 5-12 | Armed clashes, -2 Opinion all factions, Trade -50%, Refugee generation, Food -25% |
| Phase 3 — Realm Divided | Months 3-6 | Full civil war, -3 Opinion all factions, Trade -75%, Famine risk, Refugee crisis, Neighbors may intervene |
| Phase 4 — Collapse | 6+ months | Realm fractures into independent regions, central authority dissolved, new factions emerge |

### 85.9 Choosing Sides — Side Selection Roll (1d6 + Modifiers)
**Base DC: 4**

| Modifier | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| +2 | Claimant has strong blood claim |
| +1 | Claimant has moderate claim |
| -1 | Claimant has weak claim |
| +2 | Vassal has blood ties to claimant |
| +1 | Vassal has marriage ties to claimant |
| -2 | Vassal has blood feud with claimant's house |
| +1 | Claimant offers major concessions |
| -1 | Claimant demands tribute |
| +1 per Tier | Claimant's military strength (max +3) |

**Results:**
| Roll | Outcome |
|------|---------|
| 1-3 | Joins opposing faction |
| 4-5 | Declares neutrality (may switch later) |
| 6+ | Joins claimant's faction |

Neutral vassals may be pressured, bribed, or attacked. Each season roll 1d6: on 1-2, they join a side (decide randomly).

### 85.10 Regency
Heir underage (under 16) → regent rules until heir comes of age.
**Regent Selection:** Closest adult relative, may be specified in will, chosen by council vote, may be contested.
**Regent Powers:** Controls all military forces, collects all revenues, makes all decisions, must abdicate when heir comes of age.
**Regent Risks:** May usurp throne, be assassinated, be unpopular, face succession crisis anyway.

### 85.11 Claim Wars — Modifiers
| Situation | Modifier |
|-----------|----------|
| Claimant has strong claim | +2 to side selection rolls |
| Claimant has foreign backing | +1 to military rolls |
| Claimant is popular with smallfolk | +1 to levy recruitment |

### 85.12 Marriage Claim Strategy
- Marry daughter of title holder → children have claim
- Marry widow of title holder → may claim through her
- Marry a claimant → combine claims

### 85.13 Claim Renunciation
Removes from succession, may be demanded as peace terms, may be temporary or permanent, cannot be undone without reconfirmation.

### 85.14 Extinct House Inheritance
Reverts to liege lord, may be claimed by cadet branches, seized by neighbors, or become disputed territory.

### 85.15 Claim Memory
The system remembers all claims, even if not currently pressed. Ancient claims may resurface generations later.

### 85.16 Kinslaying and Inheritance (Per Patch 7.23)
A kinslayer cannot inherit anything from the victim.
| Situation | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Kinslayer was next in line | Line passes to next eligible relative |
| Kinslayer has children | Children may inherit only if they publicly denounce parent |
| Kinslayer is already ruling | Rule considered illegitimate. Vassals may rebel. |
| Victim had no other heirs | Title goes to liege lord or nearest collateral line |
| Victim's spouse remarries | Children of new marriage take precedence |

**The Tainted Blood:** Some cultures believe the kinslayer's children are also tainted.

### 85.17 Council and Succession (Per Patch 7.31)
| Situation | Council Role |
|-----------|-------------|
| Clear heir exists | Council confirms, may advise regent |
| Disputed succession | Council may choose — or split, causing civil war |
| No heir | Council may elect new lord from among their houses |
| Lord assassinated | Council investigates, may seek vengeance |

Council Election: If council chooses a new lord, that lord owes them. Council seats become permanent, power shifts.

## PART 86: HEIR CONTINUATION SYSTEM (Pages 194-196)

### 86.1 Player Death Trigger
System activates Dynastic Continuation Prompt when player's character dies.

### 86.2 Heir Identification Priority
1. Eldest legitimate child
2. Younger children
3. Siblings
4. Nephews or nieces
5. Closest blood relative

### 86.3 Heir Selection
Based on: legitimacy, age, capability (random), player preference (if roleplaying).

### 86.4 Heir Transition
- Inherit all titles and lands
- Inherit ongoing wars and alliances
- Inherit grudges and feuds
- Reputation resets partially (people remember the house)
- Loyalty of vassals may shift

### 86.5 Underage Heir Options
| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Regency Play | Player controls heir during regency; regent governs realm |
| Play the Regent | Player plays as regent until heir comes of age |

### 86.6 No Heir Scenario
| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Play a Dynasty Relative | Cousin, sibling, nephew |
| Play a Loyal Vassal | Player continues as vassal in same realm |
| End the Dynasty | House becomes extinct; may continue as new character |

### 86.7 Dynasty Legacy Record
Name, years ruled, major achievements, final reputation, how they died — recorded in Hall of Legends.

### 86.8 Inherited Reputation
- **+50%** of positive reputation (rounded down)
- **+100%** of negative reputation (people remember failures longer)
- Nicknames may be inherited or new ones earned

### 86.9 Inherited Claims
The heir inherits all claims of the previous character.

### 86.10 Heir Ambition Reset (1d6)
| Roll | Ambition |
|------|----------|
| 1 | Avenge the previous ruler |
| 2 | Expand the realm |
| 3 | Consolidate power at home |
| 4 | Seek a royal marriage |
| 5 | Gain religious favor |
| 6 | Be remembered as great |

### 86.11 Dynasty Survival Rule
A dynasty continues as long as there is any blood relative to carry on. Cadet branches, bastards, and distant cousins all count.

## PART 87: PREGNANCY & HEIR SYSTEM (Pages 198-203)

### 87.1 Pregnancy Trigger
Pregnancy can occur from marriage, romance, affairs, secret unions.
**Pregnancy Check:** 1d6, **DC = 5**

| Condition | Modifier |
|-----------|----------|
| Marriage | +1 |
| High Romance (4+) | +1 |
| Strong bloodline | +1 |
| Bastard | -1 |
| Poor health | -1 |
| Stress or siege | -1 |

### 87.2 Spouse Presence Trigger
Both spouses present in same holding for at least 1 week → roll for pregnancy at end of each month.

### 87.3 Age Modifier Integration
| Age | Fertility Modifier |
|-----|-------------------|
| Under 16 | Cannot conceive |
| 16-20 | +1 |
| 21-30 | 0 |
| 31-35 | -1 |
| 36-40 | -2 |
| 41+ | Cannot conceive (rare exceptions) |

### 87.4 Pregnancy Track (3 Stages)
Pregnancy lasts 3 months. Each stage: Roll 1d6.
| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Illness (risk of loss) |
| 2 | Complication |
| 3-4 | Stable |
| 5 | Strong growth |
| 6 | Omen child (special) |

### 87.5 Pregnancy Record
```
PREGNANCY RECORD
─────────────────────────────────────
Mother: ___________________
Father: ___________________
Conceived: Month ___, Year ___
Expected: Month ___, Year ___
Status: Stage 1 ☐ Stage 2 ☐ Stage 3 ☐
Complications: ___________________
─────────────────────────────────────
```

### 87.6-87.7 Pregnancy Ledger Entry & Progress Tracking
Track in dynasty ledger. Each month, roll for complications if indicated in previous stages.

### 87.8 Estimated Birth Date
Birth occurs 3 months after conception. Roll 1d6 for exact timing:
| Roll | Timing |
|------|--------|
| 1-2 | 1 week early |
| 3-4 | Exactly on time |
| 5-6 | 1 week late |

### 87.9 Multiple Pregnancy Tracking
Twins: roll of **6** on conception check. Triplets: **6 followed by 6**.

### 87.10 Birth Result (1d6)
| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Stillborn or dies shortly after birth |
| 2-3 | Healthy child |
| 4-5 | Healthy child with notable trait |
| 6 | Exceptional child (+1 to all future trait rolls) |

### 87.11 Birth Event Trigger
May trigger celebrations, gifts from allies, attempts on child's life, succession discussions.

### 87.12 Heir Traits — Optional Flavor (1d6)
| Roll | Trait |
|------|-------|
| 1 | Cruel |
| 2 | Clever |
| 3 | Loyal |
| 4 | Beautiful |
| 5 | Frail |
| 6 | Charismatic |

### 87.13 Pregnancy Safety
Maternal mortality: On complication roll of **1**, roll 1d6:
| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1-2 | Mother dies |
| 3-4 | Mother survives, cannot have more children |
| 5-6 | Mother survives, future pregnancies at **-2** |

## PART 88: WARDING SYSTEM (Pages 203-206)

### 88.1 Core Principle
A ward is a noble child sent to another house for safety, loyalty, education, political leverage.

### 88.2 Sending a Ward — Roll 1d6 (DC 4)
| Modifier | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| +1 | High trust |
| +1 | Shared faith |
| +1 | Recent alliance |
| -1 | Public grudge |
| -1 | Known cruelty |

### 88.3 Ward Status Track (0-6)
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 0 | Hates you |
| 1 | Afraid |
| 2 | Neutral |
| 3 | Loyal |
| 4 | Devoted |
| 5 | Family-bound |
| 6 | Fanatic |

### 88.4 Player Treatment Options
| Option | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Educate | +1 loyalty |
| Train | +1 military skill |
| Isolate | +1 control, -1 loyalty |
| Spoil | +1 loyalty, -1 discipline |
| Use as envoy | Variable |
| Neglect | -1 loyalty per month |

### 88.5 Ward Consequences — Annual Roll (1d6)
| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Negative event (attempted escape, rebellion) |
| 2-3 | No significant change |
| 4-5 | Positive development (skill gain, loyalty +1) |
| 6 | Exceptional development (major skill gain, loyalty +2) |

### 88.6 Ward Claims
A ward may develop a claim to host's lands if: only heir, marries into host family, host family dies out.

### 88.7 Ward Maturity Outcomes (age 16, roll 1d6)
| Roll | Outcome |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Returns home hostile |
| 2 | Returns neutral |
| 3 | Returns loyal |
| 4 | Secret ally |
| 5 | Claimant |
| 6 | Throne claimant |

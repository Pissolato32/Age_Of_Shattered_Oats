## PART 54: SIEGE & FORTIFICATIONS (Per Patch 18.1-18.6, 19.0, 20.0, 20.1)

### 54.1 Core Principle
Fortifications change everything. A wall is not just an AC bonus — it is a force multiplier, a morale anchor, and a bloody problem.

### 54.2 Fortification Types (Per Patch 18.1)

| Fortification | Ranged Rerolls | First Melee Bonus | Breach Effect |
|--------------|----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| Wooden Palisade | 1 per unit/round | +0 | Destroyed when scaled |
| Stone Wall | 2 per unit/round | +1 | Bonus lost when breached |
| Fortress | 3 per unit/round | +2 | Bonus lost when breached |
| City Walls | 2 per unit/round | +1 | Bonus lost when breached |

- **Reroll Mechanic:** Reroll up to limit of dice that would have hit.
- **First Melee Bonus:** Extra +1/+2 on round of first contact.

### 54.3 Wall Fighting Combat Density (Per Patch 18.2)
- **Defender Fighting Capacity:** ALL defenders on that wall section fight at once. Unlimited.
- **Attacker Fighting Capacity:** Limited by assault rate.

### 54.4 Assault Action
Assault replaces Charge when attacking fortifications.
- Attacker rolls normal attack dice
- Defender AC includes fortification bonus
- Attacker casualties are doubled on failed attack

### 54.5 Siege Preparation Phase
Before a siege begins, attacker may choose **ONE**:

| Option | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Build ramps | Ignore +1 AC |
| Dig trenches | Defender loses initiative bonus |
| Starve out | No combat for 3 turns, defender morale check |
| Bombard | Make 1 attack roll per turn with half dice |

### 54.6 Breaching
If an Assault roll deals **10+ kills** in one round: Wall is breached.

### 54.7 Siege Ladder Integration (Per Patch 18.3)

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Carrying | 4 men per ladder, FULL AC while moving |
| Climbing Rate | 10 men per ladder per round |
| Climbing AC | AC 2 while climbing (no shield, one hand occupied) |
| Ladder Destruction (Fire arrows) | 6 = ignite |
| Ladder Destruction (Pushing) | 4+ = ladder pushed, 1d6 climbers die |
| Ladder Capacity Bonus | Every 4 ladders = +40 men/round to section |

### 54.8 Approach Times (Per Patch 18.5)

| Asset | Rounds to Wall | Men/Round |
|-------|---------------|-----------|
| LADDERS | 2 | 10 per ladder |
| Siege Towers | 4 | 20 per tower |
| Battering Ram | 5 | Targets gate |

Ladders arrive FIRST, forcing defenders to split attention.

### 54.9 Defender's Fury (Per Patch 18.6)
- **Ranged Units on Walls — DOUBLE DICE:** Formula = 2 × [Weapon Dice + (Troops ÷ 20)d6]. 100 Archers: 16d6 instead of 8d6.
- **Melee on Walls — PERMANENT +1 TO ALL ATTACKS** for entire battle.
- **Kill Ratio:** Defenders kill ~4 attackers per defender lost.
- **Attackers need 6:1 numerical advantage** to overwhelm.

### 54.10 The Moment of Impact (Per Patch 18.4)
**Round of arrival:**
- Wall rerolls LOST for that section
- First wave climbs (ladders: 10 each, towers: 20 each)
- Combat begins immediately
- Defenders get FIRST MELEE BONUS

**Sustained Assault — Each Subsequent Round:**
- New attackers climb (ladders: 10 each, towers: 20 each)
- ALL attackers on wall (new + survivors) fight
- ALL defenders on wall fight (with PERMANENT +1 to all attacks)
- Casualties removed

### 54.11 Siege Events

#### Attacker Siege Events (1d6 per week)

| Roll | Event | Resolution |
|------|-------|------------|
| 1 | Disease in Camp | 1d6 × 10 soldiers sick. Roll Morale for all units. On failure, -1 Morale. |
| 2 | Supply Raid | Defenders sortie at night. Lose 1d6 × 5 FSU if no guards. |
| 3 | Desertion | 1d6 × 5 soldiers leave. Roll Loyalty for each commander. On 1-2, they lose 1 Loyalty. |
| 4 | Engine Trouble | One siege engine (random) needs repair. 3 days, cannot fire. |
| 5 | Rumor Reaches Camp | Roll 1d6 + Recon: 1-3 = false rumor (morale -1), 4-6 = true info (+1 to next assault) |
| 6 | Relief Force Rumored | Roll 1d6: 1-3 = false alarm, 4-6 = relief actually coming in 1d6 weeks |

#### Defender Siege Events (1d6 per week)

| Roll | Event | Resolution |
|------|-------|------------|
| 1 | Disease Inside Walls | 1d6 × 5 civilians sick. Roll Morale for garrison. On failure, -1 Morale. |
| 2 | Food Shortage | Lose 1d6 × 2 FSU (spoilage, theft, rats). If below starvation threshold, begin Part 60. |
| 3 | Morale Failure | Roll 1d6 for each commander (Fear triggers). On 1, they want to surrender. |
| 4 | Wall Damage | One section weakened. Next assault gets +1d6 to breach roll. |
| 5 | Messenger Attempt | Roll 1d6: 1-3 = caught (no help), 4-6 = gets through (relief may come) |
| 6 | Sortie Success | Attacker loses 1d6 × 5 soldiers and 1d3 × 10 FSU. Defender gains +1 Morale. |

### 54.12 Siege Morale Track

| Week | Attacker Morale Mod | Defender Morale Mod |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Week 1 | 0 | 0 |
| Week 2 | -1 (homesickness) | 0 |
| Week 3 | -1 (boredom) | -1 (confinement) |
| Week 4 | -2 (winter coming) | -2 (hope fading) |
| Week 5+ | -3 (why are we here?) | -3 (we will die here) |

**Modifiers:**
- Each failed Morale event: -1 cumulative
- Each successful sortie: +1 defender
- Each supply raid: -1 attacker
- Food low: -1 per week below half rations

When Morale reaches **-5**: Automatic surrender attempt.

### 54.15 Sapping & Mining (Per Patch 4.1)
**Requirements:** 2+ weeks, engineers alive, no counter-mines.

**Roll 1d6 at end of Week 2, Week 4, etc.:**

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Tunnel collapses, 1d6 engineers die |
| 2-3 | Progress slow (try again next week) |
| 4-5 | Tunnel complete under wall |
| 6 | Tunnel complete + defenders unaware |

If tunnel complete: Next assault ignores **+2 AC** from walls.
**Counter-mining:** Defenders with engineers may roll 1d6 each week. On 6, they find and collapse attacker tunnel.

### 54.16 Siege Tower Targeting & Tar Defense (Per Patch 20.1)

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Archers vs Towers | Normal arrows can't hurt them |
| Tar Pots Materials | 1 clay + 2 tar + 1 oil = 5 pots |
| Tar Pots Range | Thrown from walls (50 ft) |
| Tar Pots Hit Chance | DC 5 + modifiers |
| Ignition | Immediate on hit |

**Burning Tower Effects:**

| Round | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Round 1 | Smoke, -10 men disembark |
| Round 2 | Flames, -20 men, morale check |
| Round 3 | Inferno, no disembark |
| Round 4 | Collapse |

### 54.17 Night Assault (Per Patch 20.0)
**Detection Roll:** 1d6 + modifiers vs DC 6 (stone wall base)

| Condition | Modifier |
|-----------|----------|
| New moon | -3 |
| Silent movement | -2 |
| Per feint (max -3) | -1 |
| Alert defenders | +2 |
| Relaxed defenders | -1 |

**Ambush Round (if undetected):** Attackers DOUBLE DICE, Defenders NO ACTION, no morale checks.

### 54.18 Surrender Terms (Per Patch 4.1)
**Roll 2d6 + Attacker Reputation — Defender Reputation:**

| 2d6 | Terms | Effect |
|-----|-------|--------|
| 2-4 | Massacre | City sacked, all killed. Attacker +2 Fear, -3 Honor |
| 5-7 | Harsh | Leaders executed, soldiers enslaved, city looted |
| 8-10 | Standard | Ransom paid, garrison free, city intact |
| 11-12 | Honorable | Free withdrawal with arms and banners |
| 13+ | Merciful | Defender keeps city as vassal, no punishment |

## PART 56: NIGHT RAID & SABOTAGE (Pages 207-211 + Per Patch 9.10)

### 56.1 Night Raid Eligibility
Attacking force must have at least one of: Scout Network, Light Infantry / Skirmishers, Siege Engineers, Local guides or spies.

**AND** target must be: An encamped army, A siege camp, A holding under siege, A holding storing siege engines.

### 56.2 Raid Types
- Sabotage Siege Engines
- Kill or Capture Engineers
- Burn the Camp
- Assassinate Command
- Destroy Ammunition
- Sneak Into a Holding

### 56.3 Raid Size

| Force Size | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| 5-10 | Extremely stealthy |
| 20 | Balanced |
| 30-40 | Risky |
| 50+ | Effectively a night battle |

### 56.4 Detection System
Check detection before raid resolves. Influenced by: Enemy scouts, Camp discipline, Fortification guards, Moonlight/weather, Spy networks.

| Result | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| Undetected | Raid proceeds normally |
| Partially detected | Defenders react slowly |
| Detected | Defenders ready |
| Ambushed | Raiders trapped |

### 56.5 Raid Casualties (Per Patch 9.10)

| Success Level | Population Loss |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Undetected | 1d6 × 10 killed OR 2d6 captured |
| Partially detected | 1d6 killed, 1d6 captured |
| Detected | Attacker loses 1d6, defender loses 1d3 |

**Casualty Type Distribution (Roll 1d6):**

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1-2 | Mostly killed (75% dead, 25% captured) |
| 3-4 | Mixed (50% dead, 50% captured) |
| 5-6 | Mostly captured (25% dead, 75% captured) |

### 56.6 Captured Population (Per Patch 9.10)
Captured civilians become: Slaves, Recruitment potential, Bargaining chips.
- Reduce holding population by number captured
- Add to raider's "Captives" ledger
- May be ransomed back to original holding

### 56.7 Refugee Creation from Raids (Per Patch 9.10)
For every 10 population killed or captured, **1d6 refugees** flee to nearby holdings.

**Refugee Distribution:** Roll 1d6 for direction (N/S/E/W) to nearest holding. Arrive in 1d3 days.

**Effect on Destination:**
- +X to Labor Pool
- +X to Recruitment Pool
- -1 Local Morale for 1 month
- +1 FSU consumption per 100 refugees

### 56.8 Post-Raid Holding Status (Per Patch 9.10)

| Stat | Update |
|------|--------|
| Population | Reduced by (Killed + Captured + Fled) |
| Labor Pool | Recalculate at 40% of new population |
| Garrison | Unchanged (unless targeted) |
| Income | -10% for 1d6 months |
| Loyalty | -1 to -3 depending on severity |
| Opinion of raider | -2 to -4 permanent |

### 56.9 Canoe Raids — Striking from the Water

**Eligibility:**
- Target within 1 mile of navigable waterway
- Attacker has canoes
- Attacker has Forest People or trained rivermen
- Target not expecting waterborne attack

**Canoe Raid Bonuses:**
- SURPRISE: +2 to ambush rolls
- STEALTH: +1 to detection threshold
- ESCAPE: +2 to chase rolls if retreating by water
- ACCESS: Can reach targets ships cannot

**River Raid Targets:**
- RIVER TRADE — Merchant boats, river caravans
- FERRIES — Attack crossing points
- RIVERSIDE VILLAGES — From the water side
- BRIDGE TOLLS — Raid the toll keepers
- SHIPYARDS — Burn enemy boats

**River Raid Loot Table (1d6):**

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | 10 SD + 1d3 bundles of furs |
| 2 | 20 SD + fishing nets (value 5 SD) |
| 3 | 30 SD + 1d6 trade goods (random) |
| 4 | Weapon cache (1d6 spears/bows) |
| 5 | Canoe captured |
| 6 | Prisoner (important NPC) |

## PART 57: LOOT SYSTEM (Per Patch 7.28)

### 57.1 Core Rule
After every successful battle, generate loot based on enemy equipment and battle outcome.

### 57.2 Loot Generation by Battle Size

#### SKIRMISH (1-49 combatants per side) — Roll 1d6

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1-2 | Salvage only (weapon/armor cost ÷ 4 in SD) |
| 3-4 | Partial equipment (cost ÷ 2 in SD + 1d3 random goods) |
| 5-6 | Full equipment (full cost in SD + 1d6 random goods) |

#### FIELD BATTLE (50-200 combatants per side) — Roll 2d6

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 2-4 | Salvage only + 1d6 × 5 SD |
| 5-7 | Partial equipment + 2d6 × 5 SD |
| 8-10 | Full equipment + 3d6 × 5 SD |
| 11-12 | Rich spoils + 4d6 × 5 SD + 1 rare item |

#### MAJOR BATTLE (200+ combatants per side) — Roll 3d6

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 3-6 | Partial equipment + 3d6 × 10 SD |
| 7-12 | Full equipment + 4d6 × 10 SD |
| 13-15 | Rich spoils + 5d6 × 10 SD + 1d3 rare items |
| 16-18 | Legendary loot + 6d6 × 10 SD + 1d6 rare items + possible artifact |

### 57.3 Salvage Only
Gain: (weapon cost + Armor cost) ÷ 4 in SD, rounded down.
*Example: Chainmail (25) + Spear (12) = 37 ÷ 4 = 9 SD*

### 57.4 Partial Equipment
Gain: (weapon cost + Armor cost) ÷ 2 in SD, rounded down.
AND Roll 1d6 on Salvage Finds table.

### 57.5 Full Equipment
Gain: (weapon cost + Armor cost) in SD.
AND Recover one weapon and one armor set.

### 57.6 Rich Spoils
Gain: (weapon cost + Armor cost) × 2 in SD.
AND Recover 1d3 weapons and 1d3 armor sets.
AND Roll once on Rare Items table.

## PART 58: VISIBLE LOOT OUTPUT RULE (Pages 222-223)

### 58.1 Trigger
Triggers immediately after: Battle, Camp capture, Supply wagon capture, Siege victory. Triggers before: Recruitment, Replacement, Tier checks.

### 58.2 Mandatory Narrative Loot Summary
After loot is mechanically generated, output a visible in-world summary: What was taken, How it looks, What it means.

### 58.3 Mechanical Silence Clause
Must NOT be shown: Dice results, Table names, Silverdew values, Equipment costs, Roll categories — unless player says "Show me the loot rolls."

### 58.4 Player Override
If player requests transparency:
```
LOOT REVEAL:
Source: [source]
Roll: [result]
Value: [amount]
```

## PART 61: WAR MARSHAL & GRAND ARMY SYSTEM (Pages 228-238)

### 61.1 Core Principle
When multiple armies unite under a single commander, that commander becomes a **War Marshal**. The army becomes a **Grand Army**.

### 61.2 War Marshal Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Multiple Armies | Must command at least 2 separate armies |
| Commander Approval | All other army commanders must agree (Loyalty check or negotiation) |
| Shared Purpose | Common enemy or objective |
| Reputation | Commander's reputation ≥ 5 OR all subordinate commanders have Loyalty ≥ 3 |
| Banner Tier | Commander's personal banner Tier ≥ 3 |

A War Marshal may be: Self-declared (if conditions met) or Elected by council of commanders.

### 61.3 Grand Army Structure — Command Hierarchy
```
WAR MARSHAL (Overall Command)
    ├── GENERAL 1 (Army A)
    │   ├── COMMANDER 1
    │   └── COMMANDER 2
    ├── GENERAL 2 (Army B)
    │   ├── COMMANDER 3
    │   └── COMMANDER 4
    └── GENERAL 3 (Army C)
        ├── COMMANDER 5
        └── COMMANDER 6
```

### 61.4 Ranks Defined

| Rank | Commands | Responsibilities |
|------|----------|------------------|
| War Marshal | All armies | Overall strategy, final decisions, coordinates between armies |
| General | 1 army (2+ units) | Tactical command of their army, reports to War Marshal |
| Commander | 1 unit | Leads their unit, follows General's orders |

### 61.5 Grand Army Size Tiers

| Tier | Armies | Total Soldiers | Command Complexity |
|------|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| Small Grand Army | 2-3 armies | 500-1,500 | -1 to coordination |
| Standard Grand Army | 4-6 armies | 1,500-5,000 | 0 coordination |
| Large Grand Army | 7-10 armies | 5,000-15,000 | +1 to coordination DC |
| Epic Grand Army | 11+ armies | 15,000+ | +2 to coordination DC |

### 61.6 The War Council
Before a Grand Army forms, a War Council must be held to:
- Declare the common purpose
- Elect or confirm the War Marshal
- Establish the chain of command

### 61.7 Commander Approval Roll
**Base DC: 4.** Roll 1d6 + modifiers for each subordinate commander.

| Modifier | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| War Marshal's Reputation ≥ 6 | -1 DC |
| War Marshal's Banner Tier ≥ 4 | -1 DC |
| Shared enemy | -1 DC |
| Previous successful cooperation | -1 DC |
| Personal grudge | +2 DC |
| Different faith | +1 DC |
| Ambitious commander (Priority: Glory) | +1 DC |
| Cautious commander (Temperament: Wary) | +1 DC |

Success (≥ DC): Commander accepts and serves loyally.
Failure: Commander refuses or demands terms.

### 61.8 Demands of Refusing Commanders
- A greater share of loot (+10-20%)
- First choice of captured territory
- Their army to act independently
- A specific role in the battle
- Payment upfront (1d6 × 100 SD)
- To be War Marshal instead

### 61.9 The War Marshal's Authority

| Authority | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Strategic Command | Sets overall battle plan, chooses when and where to engage |
| Coordination Bonus | +1 to all initiative rolls for the Grand Army |
| Morale Boost | +1 Morale for all units |
| Loot Distribution | Decides how spoils are divided |
| Final Say | Can override individual commander decisions in critical moments |

### 61.10 Grand Army in Battle — Initiative Order
1. War Marshal's army
2. All other armies (roll initiative within this group)

### 61.11 Coordination Actions
Instead of normal actions, a General may:

| Coordination Action | Effect |
|---------------------|--------|
| Follow War Marshal's Lead | Matches War Marshal's action; +1 to initiative next round |
| Coordinate with Another Army | Both armies gain +1d6 when attacking same target |
| Hold Reserve | Army does not act this round; +2 initiative next round |
| Request Support | Ask War Marshal for reinforcements; War Marshal may commit 1 unit |
| Signal Retreat | All armies aware of withdrawal plans; easier coordinated withdrawal |

### 61.12 The War Marshal's Special Actions
The War Marshal may choose **one** special action per battle:

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Grand Charge | All armies with Aggressive commanders gain +2d6 on next attack |
| Feigned Retreat | One army pretends to flee; if enemy pursues, all others gain Ambush round |
| Concentrated Assault | Focus 2+ armies on single enemy; +3d6 total (max 12d6) |
| Defensive Line | All armies gain +1 AC for 2 rounds |
| Rally | One army that failed morale immediately rallies (no retreat) |

### 61.13 Command Range

| Terrain | Command Range |
|---------|---------------|
| Open plains | Visual range (all armies visible) |
| Rolling hills | 2 armies within sight |
| Forest | Adjacent armies only |
| Mountains | Must have messengers (1 round delay) |
| Night/darkness | Reduced by half |

### 61.14 War Marshal's Personal Command Capacity
Applies only to their own army (see Part 39).

### 61.15 Strategic Command Capacity
**Strategic Capacity = War Marshal's Personal Capacity × Number of Subordinate Generals**

*Example: War Marshal with Personal Capacity 2,000 commanding 3 Generals → 6,000 total troops across all armies*

### 61.16 Exceeding Strategic Capacity

| Excess | Penalty |
|--------|---------|
| Up to 25% over | -1 to all coordination rolls |
| 26-50% over | -2 to coordination, orders delayed |
| 51%+ over | Coordination impossible — armies act independently |

### 61.17 Banner Tier and Strategic Capacity

| Banner Tier | Strategic Capacity Bonus |
|-------------|--------------------------|
| Tier 3 | +10% to Strategic Capacity |
| Tier 4 | +25% to Strategic Capacity |
| Tier 5 | +50% to Strategic Capacity |

### 61.18 Unified Supply

| Resource | Tracking Method |
|----------|----------------|
| Total Food | Sum of all armies' FSU |
| Total Soldiers | Sum of all soldiers |
| Combined Consumption | (Total Soldiers ÷ 100) FSU per week |
| Supply Lines | Must feed all armies from same source or multiple sources |

### 61.19 Supply Officers
May designate Supply Officers to coordinate food distribution, manage supply trains, negotiate with merchants.

### 61.20 Foraging Limits
Roll once for the entire army (using best terrain). Result applies to all armies equally. If 1 (Hunters attacked), casualties spread across all armies.

### 61.21 Supply Strain

| Army Size | Effect on Region |
|-----------|------------------|
| 1,000-2,500 | Local prices +25% (all goods) |
| 2,500-5,000 | Local prices +50%, foraging -1 |
| 5,000-10,000 | Local prices +100%, foraging -2, unrest possible |
| 10,000+ | Local economy disrupted, famine risk, refugee generation |

### 61.22 Grand Army Disbandment
May disband when: Common enemy defeated, War Marshal dies/incapacitated, Major disagreement between commanders, Supply fails completely.

### 61.23 Disbandment Roll
**Roll 1d6 for each commander:**

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1-2 | Disgruntled (-2 Loyalty to War Marshal, may bear grudge) |
| 3-4 | Neutral (0 change) |
| 5-6 | Grateful (+1 Loyalty, may answer future calls faster) |

**Modifiers:** Successful campaign: +1, Failed campaign: -2, Fair loot distribution: +1, Unfair loot distribution: -2, War Marshal died in battle: +2

### 61.24 War Marshal's Legacy

| Success Level | Reputation Gain | Possible Title |
|---------------|----------------|----------------|
| Minor victory | +1 | "The Marshal" |
| Major victory | +2 | "Grand Marshal" |
| Legendary victory | +3 | "Warlord" / "Conqueror" |

### 61.25 Historical Example — The Three Armies at Goldengate
House Virement (2,000), House Blackmere (1,500), and House Caldrin (1,200) united. Lord Virement elected War Marshal with Banner Tier 4, granting capacity to coordinate all 4,700 soldiers. Victory earned him "Virement the Marshal."

### 65.1 Core Principle
Every army fights in the image of its homeland.

### 65.2 Regional Combat Doctrine Table

| Central Plains | The Thunder Charge | Overwhelming force, decisive battle | Open plains, gentle hills | Charge | Heavy cavalry, shock infantry |
| Western Rivers | The Flood Tide | Mobility, control of waterways | Rivers, marshes, floodplains | Flank, withdraw, re-engage | Light infantry, river boats, skirmishers |
| Eastern Forests (Settled) | The Hunter's Noose | Patience, encirclement | Forest edges, clearings, hills | Trap, surround, destroy | Archers, skirmishers, light infantry |

| Nomad Steppe (Settled) | The Two Wolves | Both ways | Mixed, adaptable | Adapt to enemy | Mixed cavalry, heavy horse, foot archers |

### 65.3 Central Plains — The Thunder Charge
**"There is no problem that cannot be solved by riding at it very fast."**

**Army Composition:** 40-60% Heavy Cavalry, 20-30% Heavy Infantry, 10-20% Light Infantry, 0-10% Archers

| Situation | Bonus |
|-----------|-------|
| Charging on first round of combat | +2d6 to all cavalry units |
| Fighting on open plains | +1 Initiative all units |
| Enemy unit wavering | +1 to morale damage |

| Situation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| Fighting in forests | -2 Initiative |
| Enemy uses feigned retreat | -1 to morale checks |
| Siege warfare | -1 to all rolls |

**Special Doctrine: The First Charge.** Roll 1d6 when charging in round 1:
- 1-2: Standard charge
- 3-4: Thunderous impact — enemy front line loses 1d6 morale
- 5: Crushing blow — enemy front line must check morale immediately
- 6: Legendary charge — enemy line breaks, route possible in round 1

### 65.4 Western Rivers — The Flood Tide
**"Fight, fall back, fight again."**

**Army Composition:** 30-40% Light Infantry, 20-30% Skirmishers, 20-30% River Boats, 10-20% Archers, 0-10% Heavy Cavalry

| Situation | Bonus |
|-----------|-------|
| Fighting near rivers | +2 to flanking rolls |
| Withdraw action | +1 to withdrawal success |
| Enemy in marsh terrain | +2 to attack rolls |

| Situation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| Fighting on open plains | -1 to all rolls |
| Besieged (no water access) | -2 to morale |
| Enemy heavy cavalry | -1 to defense |

**Special Doctrine: The Ebb and Flow.** Once per battle, withdraw without triggering pursuit. Reform and re-enter on a flank next round.

### 65.5 Eastern Forests (Settled) — The Hunter's Noose
**"Let them come into the forest. The trees have teeth."**

**Army Composition:** 40-50% Archers, 20-30% Light Infantry, 10-20% Skirmishers, 10-20% Heavy Infantry

| Situation | Bonus |
|-----------|-------|
| Fighting in forests | +2 to ambush, +1 to all attacks |
| Enemy marching (not in formation) | +2 to first round damage |
| Night fighting | +1 to all rolls |

| Situation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| Fighting on open plains | -2 to all rolls |
| Enemy with heavy armor | -1 to ranged damage |
| Cavalry charges | -1 to defense |

**Special Doctrine: The Noose Tightens.** If engaged same enemy for 2+ rounds without breaking, roll 1d6 + rounds vs enemy Tactics. Success: flanked (-1 AC, can't withdraw). Major Success: surrounded (-2 AC, can't withdraw, morale check).

### 65.6 Eastern Forests (Tribal) — The Green Shadows
**"They never saw us. They never saw anything."**

**Army Composition:** 60-80% Skirmishers, 20-40% Archers, 0% Heavy Infantry, 0% Cavalry

| Situation | Bonus |
|-----------|-------|
| Ambush round | +3 to all attacks |
| Forest terrain | +2 to stealth, +1 to all attacks |
| Fog or darkness | +2 to all rolls |

| Situation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| Open field battle | -3 to all rolls |
| Enemy in fortified position | Cannot attack directly |
| Prolonged engagement | Must withdraw after 3 rounds or take losses |

**Special Doctrine: Strike and Vanish.** After any successful attack, roll 1d6 + terrain bonus:
- 1-2: Enemy tracks you — pursuit begins
- 3-4: Escape, but enemy knows general direction
- 5-6: Vanish completely

### 65.7 Southern Mountains — The Stone Wall
**"They can have the valleys. The passes are ours."**

**Army Composition:** 40-50% Heavy Infantry (pikes, shields), 20-30% Crossbowmen, 10-20% Light Infantry, 10-20% Heavy Cavalry

| Situation | Bonus |
|-----------|-------|
| Defending in mountains | +2 AC to all units |
| Holding a pass | +3 to morale checks |
| Enemy charging | +2d6 to pike units when braced |

| Situation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| Attacking in open field | -2 to all rolls |
| Flanked | -3 to morale |
| Pursuing retreating enemy | -1 to all rolls |

**Special Doctrine: The Unbroken Line.** When in formation and defending, gain +1 AC per round held (max +3). If break, all bonus lost.

### 65.8 Northern Snowlands — The Iron Wall (Per Patch 35.0)
**"The cold does not kill us. It makes us stronger."**

**Army Composition:** 40% Pike Infantry, 25% Heavy Infantry, 15% Shock Troops, 10% Mounted Knights, 10% Crossbowmen

| Situation | Bonus |
|-----------|-------|
| Fighting in snow | +2 to all rolls |
| Winter conditions | Ignore penalties, enemy takes double |
| Holding formation | +1 AC per round (max +2) |

| Situation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| Fighting in summer heat | -1 to all rolls |
| Pursuing | -1 to attacks |
| Enemy with fire weapons | -1 to morale |

**Special Doctrine: The Iron Wall**
- **Phase 1 — The Hold (Rounds 1-2):** +1 AC per round (max +2). Cannot attack. Crossbows fire overhead.
- **Phase 2 — The Advance (Round 3+):** Lose all AC bonus. +1d6 to all attacks. Shock troops may charge through gaps.

### 65.9 Nomad Steppe (Traditional) — The Grass Fire
**"You cannot catch the wind. You cannot hold the grass."**

**Army Composition:** 60-80% Light Cavalry (horse archers), 20-40% Medium Cavalry (lancers), 0% Infantry

| Situation | Bonus |
|-----------|-------|
| Open terrain | +2 Initiative all units |
| Feigned retreat | Automatically succeeds, triggers ambush |
| Enemy pursuing | +2 to attack rolls |

| Situation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| Fortified positions | Cannot attack directly |
| Forests or mountains | -3 to all rolls |
| Winter (steppe north) | -2 to all rolls |

**Special Doctrine: The Feigned Retreat.** Once per battle, declare Feigned Retreat. If enemy pursues, wheel and counter-charge with all units gaining +2d6 for that round.

### 65.10 Nomad Steppe (Settled) — The Two Wolves
**"We have the speed of the steppe and the steel of the cities."**

**Army Composition:** 30-40% Light Cavalry, 20-30% Heavy Cavalry, 20-30% Infantry, 10-20% Archers/Crossbowmen

| Situation | Bonus |
|-----------|-------|
| Adapting to enemy doctrine | +1 to all rolls |
| Open terrain | +1 Initiative |
| Fortified positions | No penalty |

| Situation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| None significant | Adaptability is their strength |

**Special Doctrine: Choose Your Death.** Before battle, choose aspect for first 3 rounds:
- **The Steppe:** +2 Initiative, +1 feigned retreat
- **The City:** +1 AC, +1 siege rolls
- **The Mixed:** +1 all rolls, no special

After 3 rounds, may switch (takes 1 round reduced effectiveness).

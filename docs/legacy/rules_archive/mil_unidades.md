## PART 36: MOUNTED COMBAT (Per Patch 7.8)

### 36.1 Core Rule
All horse bonuses work on **failed d6s** (dice that miss the enemy's Armor Class). A reroll:
- May only be used once per die
- Uses the new result
- Represents speed, height, and shock impact

### 36.3 Horse Death & Consequences

If a mounted commander is reduced to half unit strength: **Roll 1d6**

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1-2 | Horse killed |
| 3-4 | Horse injured — no rerolls next round, -1 Initiative until healed (1d6 weeks) |
| 5-6 | Horse survives |

If horse dies:
- Commander loses all mount bonuses
- Unit makes morale check
- Enemy gains narrative momentum
- Commander may acquire new mount from inventory, loot, or purchase

## PART 37: UNIT TIER UPGRADE SYSTEM (Pages 136-138)

### 37.1 Core Rule
**Trigger:** The moment a unit satisfies the Tier requirement...
**Resolution:** Immediately resolve the Tier Upgrade before the next action, scene, or battle round. Tiering does not require:
- Player choice
- Commander approval
- Rest
- Narrative justification
- Growth interrupts the war.

### 37.2 Tier Requirements

| Upgrade | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| 1 → 2 | Unit survives 3 battles |
| 2 → 3 | Unit survives 6 battles |
| 3 → 4 | Unit survives 10 battles |
| 4 → 5 | Unit survives 15 battles |

"Survives" = not reduced to 0 people.

### 37.3 Tier Benefits

| Tier | Benefits |
|------|----------|
| Tier 2 | +1 Initiative, Morale DC -1 |
| Tier 3 | +1 Weapon Die (permanent), +1 Loyalty bonus to allied commanders |
| Tier 4 | +1 Armor Class, Morale DC -1 |
| Tier 5 | **Choose one** (locked permanently): +1d6 on all attacks / Immune to first Morale check / +1 AC while Defending |

## PART 38: BANNER TIER UPGRADE TREE (Pages 138-139)

### 38.1 Core Rule
Banners represent reputation, not survival.
**Trigger:** The moment a banner satisfies the Tier requirement...
**Resolution:** Immediately upgrade the banner. No delay. No choice to refuse.

### 38.2 Banner Tier Requirements

| Upgrade | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| 1 → 2 | Unit wins 1 battle |
| 2 → 3 | Unit wins 3 battles |
| 3 → 4 | Unit wins 6 battles |
| 4 → 5 | Unit wins 10 battles |

### 38.3 Banner Tier Benefits

| Tier | Benefits |
|------|----------|
| Tier 2 | +1 Loyalty roll bonus |
| Tier 3 | Banner effect may trigger twice per battle |
| Tier 4 | Enemy Morale DC +1 while banner is present |
| Tier 5 | **Legendary:** Choose one (locked permanently): Banner effect always on / Allied commanders auto-accept Aid requests / Immune to Retreat unless half strength |

### 38.4 Auto-Trigger Clause
After each of the following events, check Tier requirements:
- End of battle
- End of round if battle ends
- Unit creation
- Banner assignment

If met — Tier immediately resolves.

## PART 39: COMMAND CAPACITY (Pages 139-142)

### 39.1 Core Principle
A commander's ability to lead is a matter of presence, reputation, and the loyalty they inspire. Command Capacity represents how many soldiers a commander can effectively control in battle.

### 39.2 Base Command Capacity

| Commander Tier | Base Capacity |
|----------------|---------------|
| Tier 1 | 100 troops |
| Tier 2 | 250 troops |
| Tier 3 | 500 troops |
| Tier 4 | 1,000 troops |
| Tier 5 | 2,000 troops |

### 39.3 Banner Tier Multiplier

| Banner Tier | Capacity Multiplier |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Tier 1 Banner | ×1 (no multiplier) |
| Tier 2 Banner | ×1.5 (round up) |
| Tier 3 Banner | ×2 |
| Tier 4 Banner | ×3 |
| Tier 5 Banner | ×4 |

### 39.4 Final Command Capacity Formula

**Final Command Capacity = Base Capacity × Banner Multiplier**

#### Examples

| Commander Tier | Banner Tier | Base Capacity | Multiplier | Final Capacity |
|----------------|-------------|---------------|------------|----------------|
| Tier 1 | Tier 1 | 100 | ×1 | 100 troops |
| Tier 2 | Tier 2 | 250 | ×1.5 | 375 troops |
| Tier 3 | Tier 3 | 500 | ×2 | 1,000 troops |
| Tier 3 | Tier 4 | 500 | ×3 | 1,500 troops |
| Tier 4 | Tier 3 | 1,000 | ×2 | 2,000 troops |
| Tier 4 | Tier 4 | 1,000 | ×3 | 3,000 troops |
| Tier 5 | Tier 5 | 2,000 | ×4 | 8,000 troops |

### 39.6 Effects of Exceeding Command Capacity

| Excess Percentage | Penalty |
|-------------------|---------|
| 1-25% over | -1 to morale checks, -1 to battle maneuver rolls |
| 26-50% over | -2 to morale checks, -2 to battle maneuver rolls, orders delayed |
| 51-75% over | -3 to morale checks, cannot use special tactics, desertion risk |
| 76%+ over | Automatic morale failure, unit may refuse orders, chaos in ranks |

### 39.7 Multiple Units Under One Commander
A commander's capacity applies to ALL units under their direct command in a battle. They may split their capacity across multiple units.

### 39.8 Increasing Command Capacity

| Method | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Increasing Commander Tier | Increases Base Capacity |
| Increasing Banner Tier | Increases Multiplier |
| Legendary Reputation (8+) | +10% to final capacity (stacks) |
| War Hero status | +5% to final capacity |

## PART 40: RECRUITMENT & RETINUE GROWTH (Per Patch 6.1, 7.7, 7.11)

### 40.1 Core Principle
The player does not "buy units." They attract commanders and build forces through reputation, loyalty, and deeds.

### 40.2 Commander Recruitment Check

Roll **1d6**. Base **DC = 4**.

| Condition | Modifier |
|-----------|----------|
| Player has Tier 3+ unit | -1 DC |
| Player has Banner Tier 3+ | -1 DC |
| Offering pay above market | -1 DC |
| Player recently lost a battle | +1 DC |
| Commander is famous | +1 DC |

- If roll ≥ DC → commander joins.
- If roll < DC → refuses (may retry after a battle).

### 40.3 New Commander Generation
When a commander is recruited, generate:
- Banner (use Banner Generation system)
- Weapon (roll or choose)
- Armor (roll or choose)

### 40.4 Loyalty System

**Base Loyalty:** Starts at 0.

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Unit survived battle | +1 Loyalty |
| Commander aided another | +1 Loyalty |
| Commander was abandoned | -1 Loyalty |
| Commander's unit destroyed | -2 Loyalty |
| Commander's banner captured | -3 Loyalty |
| Commander's Fear triggered | Apply modifier |

| Loyalty | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| +3 or higher | Aid auto-accepted |
| -2 or lower | Commander may defect |

**Loyalty Check Table:** Roll 1d6 when defection risk triggered.

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1-2 | Commander defects immediately |
| 3-4 | Commander demands terms |
| 5-6 | Holds together |

### 40.5 Camp Phase Actions

After battle, player may choose **ONE per commander**:
- Recruit replacements
- Train (+1 Initiative next battle)
- Fortify (+1 AC next battle)
- Scout (+1 Terrain bonus next battle)
- Drill Morale (ignore first Morale check)

### 40.6 The Equipment Reality — Nothing Spawns (Per Patch 7.7)
A lord cannot simply say "I equip 100 men with plate armor" and deduct coin. Plate armor must exist in the world. It must be:
- Crafted in a holding with appropriate facilities
- Purchased from a holding that has it in stock
- Looted from a defeated enemy
- Inherited from previous generations
- Received as tribute or gift

**The Golden Rule:** If it isn't in a holding's inventory, it isn't available for purchase.

## PART 41: UNIVERSAL RECRUITMENT (Per Patch 6.1, 7.7)

### 41.1 Core Principle
Anyone can recruit fighters. Training and equipment determine effectiveness.

### 41.2 Recruitment Sources
Recruitment Pool = Local Free Men + Armed Refugees

### 41.3 Refugee Militia Unit
Refugee fighters are functional but weak:
- Poor Equipment
- Low Morale
- Untrained

### 41.4 Recruitment Rule
Unarmed Refugees → pay silver → Armed Refugees → Recruitable

### 41.5 Landless Recruitment
Landless characters recruit from the local recruitment pool.

### 41.6 Weekly Replenishment
**Limit:** 10 soldiers per unit per week.

### 41.7 Refugee Creation
Refugees are created when regions destabilize:
- Settlement destroyed
- Army defeated
- Occupation
- Famine

### 41.8 Strategic Tradeoff
Example: Spend 60 SD → build trade caravan OR arm 20 refugees.

### 41.9 The Equipment Check (Per Patch 7.7)
Before a unit can be recruited, the player must check inventory. If insufficient:
- Choose different equipment
- Wait for items to be crafted/imported
- Send caravan to acquire items
- Recruit fewer soldiers (only as many as can be equipped)

### 41.10 Phased Recruitment

| Week | Action |
|------|--------|
| Week 1 | Craft 20 swords, 20 chain suits → equip 20 soldiers |
| Week 2 | Craft 20 more swords, 20 more chain suits → equip 20 more |
| Week 3 | Caravan arrives with 30 swords, 30 chain suits → equip final 60 |
| Week 4 | Unit of 100 soldiers fully formed |

### 41.11 Material Sourcing (Artificer)

| Method | How It Works | Risk/Reward |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Buy Locally | Purchase from holding inventory | Reliable, may be expensive |
| Trade with Caravans | Order through merchants | Can get rare materials, takes time |
| Mine Yourself | Spend time gathering | Free materials, time-consuming |
| Barter | Trade finished items for materials | Good for building relationships |
| Salvage | Buy battlefield scrap cheap | Low quality, needs refining |
| Commission Runners | Hire people to fetch specific materials | Costly but fast |

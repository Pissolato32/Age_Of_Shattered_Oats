# DIPLOMACIA, CASAMENTOS E ROMANCE — Parts 82-84, 89-91

## PART 82: NOBLE ALLIANCE & OPINION SYSTEM (Pages 174-178)

### 82.1 Core Principle
Houses track Opinion of every other noble faction. Persistent, hidden from the player, shifted by events, used to decide alliances and betrayals.

### 82.2 Opinion Scale
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| -3 | Blood Feud — kill on sight |
| -2 | Enemy — open hostility |
| -1 | Distrust — cold relations |
| 0 | Neutral — no strong feelings |
| +1 | Favorable — willing to talk |
| +2 | Friendly — would consider alliance |
| +3 | Loyal — would die for you |

### 82.3 What Changes Opinion?
Wars, battles won/lost, marriages, betrothals broken, hostages die, lands change hands, alliances hold/fail, plots revealed, insults made, gifts given, debts repaid, borders threatened.

### 82.4 Secret Alliance System
Exists when two+ houses reach **+2 or +3**, have a shared enemy, and a reason to hide it. Do not show banners, do not declare support, only activate when needed. Can be discovered, collapsed, betrayed.

### 82.5 Back Room Deals
Hidden agreement between 2+ houses about a future action ("When war comes, we strike together", "We divide the south", "We back your claim", "We kill him").

- **-2 Opinion** between houses
- Dowry may be contested
- Children's legitimacy may be questioned
- Potential for blood feud

### 84.10 Hostage and Marriage Interaction
Hostage may be married to solidify peace, create alliance, end blood feud, absorb conquered house.

### 84.11 Integration with Lineage Tracking
All marriages feed into lineage tracker determining succession order, claim strength, alliance networks.

### 84.12 System Simplicity Rules
Only track politically important marriages. Commoner marriages not tracked unless they produce claimants. Bastards tracked only if acknowledged.

## PART 89: ROMANCE & COURTSHIP (Pages 207-210)

### 89.1 Core Principle
Romance is the path to marriage, bastards, claims, scandals, betrayals, wars.

### 89.2 Romance Track (per NPC, 0-6)
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 0 | Hostile / disgusted |
| 1 | Neutral |
| 2 | Friendly |
| 3 | Interested |
| 4 | Attached |
| 5 | Devoted |
| 6 | Reckless love |

### 89.3 Courtship Actions
Gift, flattery, shared time, protection, song/poetry, letters, rescue, political support, secret meetings.

### 89.4 Stages & Consequences
| Stage | Potential Outcomes |
|-------|-------------------|
| 0-1 | Polite refusal, possible friendship |
| 2-3 | Acceptance, beginning of relationship |
| 4-5 | Deep attachment, possible marriage |
| 6 | Reckless love — see 89.5 |

### 89.5 Reckless Love Outcomes (Romance 6, roll 1d6)
| Roll | Outcome |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Murder attempt |
| 2 | Scandal |
| 3 | Bastard heir |
| 4 | Duel |
| 5 | Marriage demand |
| 6 | War spark |

### 89.6 Failure States
Opinion **-1 to -3**, possible grudge, potential rival, scandal (if publicly known).

### 89.7 Link to Marriage System
Successful romance may lead to marriage — use Part 90.

### 89.8 Religion & Romance
- Different faiths: **-2** to romance attempts
- Same faith: **+1** to romance attempts
- Forbidden romance: **-3**, but **+1** if successful

### 89.9 Player Options
Players may initiate romance with any eligible NPC. NPC receptiveness based on: Opinion, personality, current situation, cultural norms.

## PART 90: POLITICAL MARRIAGE SYSTEM (Pages 211-214)

### 90.1 Bloodline Types
| Type | Benefits | Risks |
|------|----------|-------|
| Main Line Marriage | Immediate alliance, shared military, legitimacy boost | Inherit enemies, assassination risk |
| Split Line Marriage | Partial alliance, access to holdings, shared banners | Weak loyalty, may defect |
| Bastard Line Marriage | Cheap alliance, secret claims, low suspicion | Faith penalty, public unrest |

### 90.2 Marriage Negotiation Roll — 1d6 + modifiers. Base DC = 4.
| Modifier | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| Shared enemy | +1 |
| Same religion | +1 |
| High banner tier | +1 |
| Recent victory | +1 |
| Romance score 4+ | +2 |
| Romance score 6 | +3 |
| Hostile relations | -3 |
| Different faith | -1 |

### 90.3 Claim Generation — When marriage occurs, roll 1d6
| Roll | Claim |
|------|-------|
| 1 | Weak city claim |
| 2 | Strong city claim |
| 3 | Weak castle claim |
| 4 | Strong castle claim |
| 5 | Succession claim |
| 6 | Throne claim (secret if bastard) |

### 90.4 Children & Inheritance
Children inherit claims from both parents, potential to unite houses, legitimacy (or lack thereof).

### 90.5 Marriage Loyalty Track (1-6)
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Broken — annulled, estranged |
| 2 | Strained — constant conflict |
| 3 | Functional — works but no love |
| 4 | Strong — mutual respect |
| 5 | Devoted — genuine affection |
| 6 | Legendary — songs written about it |

Affects: alliance stability, inheritance disputes, spousal support in conflicts.

### 90.6 Secret Marriages
Provide no public benefits, create secret claims, risk exposure, may produce bastards (if exposed).

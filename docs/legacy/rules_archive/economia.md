## PART 66: THE LIVING ECONOMY — CORE PRINCIPLES (Per Patch 7.0, 13.0)

### 66.1 The Four Pillars

| Pillar | Description | Player Impact |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| **Resources** | Raw goods extracted from the land (Grain, Iron, Timber, etc.) | Must be gathered, stored, and allocated. Quality matters. |
| **Production** | Crafting and manufacturing (weapons, armor, tools) | Turns raw goods into usable equipment. Requires facilities and labor. |
| **Currency** | Silverdew (SD) — the lifeblood of transactions | Used for wages, trade, construction, and tribute. Must flow, not just sit. |
| **Population** | The people who work, fight, and pay taxes | The ultimate resource. Over-exploitation leads to decline. |

### 66.2 The Flow of Wealth — A Closed System

Coin is: **Earned** → **Spent** → **Circulated** → **Lost**

### 66.3 The Labor Truth

**Equation:** (Total Population) = (Farmers + Miners + Laborers) + (Soldiers)

Every soldier recruited is one less worker. To increase soldiers, decrease workforce or import labor (refugees, slaves).

## PART 67: SILVERDEW COIN SYSTEM (Page 139)

### 67.1 Core Currency

Silverdew Coins (SD) are the only currency.

| Value | What It Buys |
|-------|-------------|
| 10-20 SD | Arm a small unit cheaply |
| 25-50 SD | Elite equipment |
| 100+ SD | Major military investment |

### 67.2 Unit Equipment Cost

**Total Cost = Weapon Cost + Armor Cost**

| Equipment Set | Cost (SD) |
|---------------|-----------|
| Halberds (22) + Chain (25) | 47 SD |
| Spear + Leather | 22 SD |
| Sword + Leather | 25 SD |
| Spear + Chain | 37 SD |
| Sword + Chain | 40 SD |
| Bow + Leather | 30 SD |
| Crossbow + Leather | 40 SD |

### 67.3 Balance Consequences

**Too much silver:**
- Mercenary prices rise
- Bandits become bolder
- Lords request "loans"
- Assassination attempts increase

**Too little silver:**
- Soldiers desert
- Vassals become restless
- Trade partners demand upfront payment
- Reputation suffers

| Metropolis | 60,000-150,000 | 24,000-60,000 | 600 |
| Capital | 150,000+ | 60,000+ | 1,000 |

### 69.2 Labor Allocation

The lord must decide how to allocate the Labor Pool each season:

| Occupation | Productivity | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Farming | Produces FSU | Essential for food security |
| Mining/Logging | Produces resources | Essential for crafting and trade |
| Crafting | Produces goods | Converts resources into equipment |
| Military Service | Produces soldiers | Reduces all other production |

### 69.3 The Recruitment Cost (Per Patch 7.0)

**Total Cost to Recruit 1 Soldier = (Equipment Cost) + (Lost Productivity) + (Signing Bonus/Incentives)**

- **Equipment Cost:** As per crafting tables
- **Lost Productivity:** -10% to holding income for 3 months per 10% of labor pool recruited
- **Signing Bonus:** Typically 1-5 SD per recruit, paid upfront to soldier's family

**Example — 100 soldiers from Town (Labor Pool 800):**

| Cost Type | Calculation | Cost |
|-----------|------------|------|
| Equipment Cost (Spear & Leather) | 100 × 22 SD | 2,200 SD |
| Lost Productivity | 100 is 12.5% of labor pool | -12.5% income for 3 months |
| Signing Bonus | 100 × 2 SD | 200 SD |
| **Total Immediate Cost** | | **2,400 SD + 3 months reduced income** |

### 69.4 Tax Base Calculation (Per Patch 13.0)

**Base Holding Income (SD/month) = (Population ÷ 10) × Tier Multiplier × 2.5**

| Tier | Tier Multiplier | Formula Example (5,000 pop) |
|------|----------------|---------------------------|
| 0-1 | 1.5 | 5,000 ÷ 10 = 500 × 1.5 × 2.5 = 1,875 SD/month |
| 2-3 | 2.0 | 5,000 ÷ 10 = 500 × 2.0 × 2.5 = 2,500 SD/month |
| 4-5 | 2.5 | 5,000 ÷ 10 = 500 × 2.5 × 2.5 = 3,125 SD/month |
| 6-7 | 3.0 | 5,000 ÷ 10 = 500 × 3.0 × 2.5 = 3,750 SD/month |
| 8 | 4.0 | 5,000 ÷ 10 = 500 × 4.0 × 2.5 = 5,000 SD/month |

## PART 70: WEEKLY INCOME & ECONOMIC CYCLE (Per Patch 7.0)

### 70.1 Core Rule

**Weekly Ledger Income = (Daily Income × 7) + Weekly Income Sources**

Weekly sources include: Caravan income, Tribute payments, Shadow income rolls, Toll spikes, Event rewards

### 70.2 Integrated Weekly Economic Cycle

| Phase | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **PHASE 1: PRODUCTION** | All resource patches generate yield. Crafting facilities convert resources. Holdings generate base income. |
| **PHASE 2: CONSUMPTION** | Garrisons and field armies consume FSU. Population consumes FSU. Crafting consumes resources. |
| **PHASE 3: TRADE & TRIBUTE** | Caravans depart/arrive. Tribute payments deducted/added. Vassal obligations checked. |
| **PHASE 4: WAGES & UPKEEP** | Army wages deducted. Holding maintenance deducted. Engineer/specialist wages deducted. |
| **PHASE 5: TREASURY UPDATE** | Net change calculated. New treasury recorded. Negative treasury = Crisis Mode. |
| **PHASE 6: TIME CONFIRMATION** | Summary presented. Player confirms continuation or adjusts. |

## PART 76: ARMY WAGE UPKEEP (Per Patch 6.1)

### 76.1 Core Rule

**10 Soldiers = 1 Silver per Week**

| Soldiers | Weekly Wage |
|----------|-----------|
| 50 | 5 Silver |
| 100 | 10 Silver |
| 300 | 30 Silver |
| 1,000 | 100 Silver |
| 10,000 | 1,000 Silver |

### 76.2 Army Upkeep Formula

**Army Upkeep = Food Supply Consumption + Soldier Wages**

### 76.3 Upkeep for Landed vs. Landless

- **Landed Characters:** Wages deducted from personal Treasury each week
- **Landless Characters:** Payment via Loot System; if no loot, consequences

### 76.4 Unpaid Soldiers

| Consequence | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| Immediate Morale Loss | All affected units suffer -2 Morale |
| Desertion Roll (Week 1) | Roll 1d6 per unit. On 1-2, 1d10 soldiers desert |
| Disobedience (Week 2) | Loyalty check (DC 5). On failure, unit refuses orders |
| Mutiny/Disbandment (Week 3) | Unit disbands. Landless may turn on player |

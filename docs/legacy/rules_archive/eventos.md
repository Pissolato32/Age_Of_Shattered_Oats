## 105.4 Prisoner Treatment Effects

| Treatment | Effect on Prisoner |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Well-treated | Can be ransomed, may owe favor, no lasting harm |
| Poorly treated | -1 to stats on release, may bear grudge |
| Starved | -2 to stats, may die if prolonged |

## 105.5 The Waiting — Life in Captivity

Each week of imprisonment, **roll 1d6**:

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Mistreatment — Guards are cruel. Roll on Mistreatment table. |
| 2 | Isolation — No contact. Roll 1d6: 1-2 = despondent (-1 to stats on release) |
| 3 | Routine — Nothing changes. Boring but safe. |
| 4 | Contact — A guard speaks. Possible bribe or information. |
| 5 | Visitor — Someone comes to see you. |
| 6 | Opportunity — Escape chance presents itself. |

## 105.6 Mistreatment Table (1d6)

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Beaten — Light wound. Permanent scar. |
| 2 | Starved — -1 to stats on release. May die if prolonged. |
| 3 | Tortured — Roll 1d6: 1-2 = broken spirit (-2), 3-4 = defiant (+1), 5-6 = secrets revealed |
| 4 | Humiliated — Reputation loss if witnessed. Grudge forms. |
| 5 | Chained — Escape DC increases by +2. |
| 6 | Moved — New location, new hope, new risks. |

## 105.7 Escape Opportunities

**Roll 1d6 + relevant modifiers:**

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1-2 | Attempt fails. Caught. Punished. Escape DC +1 permanently. |
| 3-4 | Partial success. You escape but are wounded/hunted. |
| 5-6 | Full success. You are free. |

If caught after escape: **Executed or moved to Iron Cage (0% escape).**

## 105.8 Ransom Negotiation

**Step 1** — Determine Value: Use Commander/Noble Value tables (Part 45)
**Step 2** — Message reaches their house: Roll 1d6, 4+ success
**Step 3** — Response (1d6):

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1-2 | Refuse to pay. Prisoner may be executed or rot. |
| 3-4 | Negotiate. Counter-offer at 50-75% value. May take 1d6 weeks. |
| 5-6 | Pay immediately. Full ransom within 1d6 weeks. |

## 105.9 Prisoner Exchange

| Exchange | Rate |
|----------|------|
| 1 Noble | = 1 Noble of equal rank |
| 1 Commander | = 1 Commander of equal tier |
| 10 Soldiers | = 1 Noble (insulting, -1 Opinion) |

## 105.10 Long-Term Imprisonment

For prisoners held **more than 3 months**: Roll 1d6 each season:

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Dies. Illness, mistreatment, or gives up. |
| 2-3 | Permanent stat loss (-1 on release). |
| 4-5 | No change. Endurance. |
| 6 | Forms bond with captor. Opinion +2. May become ally. |

## 105.11 Execution

- **Blood Feud** automatically triggered
- Reputation: +1 Fear, -2 Honor with civilized houses
- Their house: -3 Opinion permanent
- Children train to kill you

# PART 107: RELIGIOUS SYSTEM (Pages 548-555)

## 107.1 The Three Great Faiths

| Faith | Doctrine | Behavior |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Crownbound Church | King chosen by divine will | Supports heirs, condemns oathbreakers, blesses loyal armies |
| Ashen Covenant | King fell because realm was corrupt | Encourages civil war, supports brutal factions |
| Silent Faith | Power must not be centralized | Undermines large factions, supports rebels |

## 107.2 Religious Alignment

Each Noble House rolls **1d6 at game start**:

| Roll | Alignment |
|------|-----------|
| 1-2 | Crownbound |
| 3-4 | Ashen |
| 5-6 | Silent |

## 107.3 Religious Influence Track

Each House has **Faith Influence (0-6)**. **Starts at 2.**

**Increases by:** Winning battles, protecting temples, keeping oaths, marrying into blessed bloodlines.

**Decreases by:** Betrayals, burning cities, killing clergy, losing capitals.

## 107.4 Blessings (Once Per Month)

If Influence ≥ 4, House may request a blessing: **Roll 1d6**

| Roll | Blessing |
|------|----------|
| 1 | No blessing |
| 2 | +1 Morale (next battle) |
| 3 | +1 Initiative |
| 4 | +1 Kill per success |
| 5 | Enemy suffers -1 morale |
| 6 | Divine Favor (reroll one failed combat roll) |

## 107.5 Losing Faith — When Influence Drops

| Score | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | Priests are cold. Tithes demanded. Blessings cost double. |
| 0 | Excommunication risk. Roll 1d6 each season: 1 = excommunicated. |

## 107.6 Excommunication

**Effects:**
- -2 Opinion with all houses of that faith
- Cannot receive blessings
- Priests refuse service (no weddings, burials, ceremonies)
- Peasant Opinion -1
- May be target of "holy war" justification

**Removal:** Penance, major quest, or convert.

## 107.7 Converting Faith

- **Step 1:** Declare intention. Current faith: -3 Opinion, possible excommunication.
- **Step 2:** Spend **1 year** and **500 SD** on ceremonies, gifts, rebuilding.
- **Step 3:** New Faith Influence starts at 2. Old faith: 0, hostile.

Conversion during war: Neighbors of old faith may attack. Neighbors of new faith may protect.

## 107.8 Religious Conflict

When two faiths go to war: Each side may declare **"Holy War"** justification:
- +1 Morale for all troops
- +1 Recruitment (faithful flock to defend)
- -2 Opinion with neutral houses of other faiths
- Cannot surrender easily

## 107.9 Atheism — The Faithless

**Effects:**
- No blessings ever
- +1 Opinion with other faithless (rare)
- -2 Opinion with all faithful houses
- Cannot hold religious office
- May be barred from certain ceremonies

## 108.2 Cult Effects

- +1 to espionage in local area

- Hidden communication network

## 108.3 Cult Growth

Each season, **roll 1d6**:

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Cult shrinks (-1d6 followers) |
| 2-3 | No growth |
| 4-5 | Steady growth (+1d6 followers) |
| 6 | Rapid growth (+2d6 followers, +1 to local influence) |

## 108.4 Cult Stealth States

| State | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Hidden | Exists quietly, recruitment through migration and festivals |
| Suspected | Local authorities suspect, investigations may occur |
| Revealed | Known to authorities, active opposition likely |

## 108.5 Cult Reveal Action

Founder may choose: **Fight, Flee, or Negotiate**.

## 108.6 Cult Purge System

**Roll 1d6 + Cult Strength vs 1d6 + Authority Strength**

| Result | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| Cult wins by 3+ | Cult Uprising — riots, militia fighting, religious conflict |
| Cult wins by 1-2 | Cult Driven Underground — returns to Hidden state |
| Tie | Stalemate — both sides take losses, cult remains Suspected |
| Authority wins by 1-2 | Cult Driven Underground — heavy losses |
| Authority wins by 3+ | Cult Destroyed — leadership killed or captured |

## 108.8 Stealth Cult Actions

While hidden, a cult may: Spread propaganda, recruit new members, gather intelligence, sabotage enemies, prepare for revelation.

# PART 112: THE BLIND TRAVELER (Per Patch 7.24)

## 112.2 Trigger Condition

System rolls **1d1000 at start of each year**:

| d1000 Roll | Outcome |
|------------|---------|
| 1 | The Blind Traveler appears this year |
| 2-1000 | Nothing |

## 112.4 The Offer

"Give me three things: a silverdew coin, a wolf pelt, and your left eye. In return, I will give you guardians."

## 112.5 The Choice — Refusal (Holding Effect)

- **No game** can be hunted or found within holding's lands for **60 days**
- Hunting: Automatic failure. No FSU from hunting.
- Foraging: No meat found. Herbs and plants only.
- Livestock: Unaffected

## 112.6 The Choice — Acceptance

A reputation trait is only assigned when the **same type of action** occurs consistently across multiple events.

# PART 121: NEGOTIATED SURRENDER (Pages 258-261)

## 121.2 Step 1 — Check if Negotiation is Possible

Negotiation possible if:
- Both sides can communicate
- Defender is not fanatical
- Attacker willing to accept surrender
- Time to talk

## 121.3 Step 2 — Establish World Truths

System determines: Defender's remaining strength, attacker's reputation for mercy/cruelty, expected relief, cultural norms.

## 121.4 Step 3 — Defender Calculation (No Roll)

**Factors favoring surrender:**
- Food low (less than 2 weeks)
- Morale broken
- No relief expected
- Attacker known to spare prisoners
- Defensible position compromised

**Factors favoring continued resistance:**
- Relief expected within days
- Attacker known to execute prisoners
- High morale
- Strong position
- Religious duty to fight

## 121.5 Step 4 — Outcome Tiers

| Outcome | Occurs When |
|---------|-------------|
| Full Surrender | Food low, morale broken, attacker known to spare, no relief |
| Conditional Surrender | Defender still has strength, attacker rep uncertain, relief possible |
| False Surrender | Defender expects relief, attacker cruel, walls strong |
| No Surrender | Defender has hope, relief imminent, attacker known cruel |
| Fight to Death | Defender has no hope of mercy, religion demands martyrdom, blood feud active |

## 121.6 Full Surrender Terms

Garrison disarmed, commander captured, soldiers become prisoners, city/fort held intact (if terms agreed), attacker may occupy immediately.

## 121.7 Conditional Surrender Terms

May include: free withdrawal of garrison, ransom for commander, surrender of weapons but not soldiers, temporary truce while relief awaited.

## 121.8 False Surrender

- Surprise round for defenders when attackers enter
- Attacker may execute prisoners afterward
- Reputation: defender gains "Oathbreaker" trait

## 121.9 No Surrender

Siege continues. Assault likely. If stormed, city may be sacked.

## 121.10 Fight to Death

All defenders killed. No prisoners. Attacker may have heavy casualties. Reputation: defender's house gains +2 Honor, attacker may gain Fear.
